# Keyword research snapshot

## Scope

Current evidence covers Worldwide English queries collected around 2026-09-03 to 2026-10-03. Treat it as a dated research snapshot, not permanent truth.

## Current channel decision

The channel has selected `vintage jazz` as its stable main genre and default primary keyword because it best matches the Popeye character, mid-century visual language and intended sound. `late night jazz` is the recurring secondary branch. `jazz noir` is a conditional secondary branch for genuinely noir episodes. Future research should optimize how these terms are deployed, not silently replace the chosen genre identity.
